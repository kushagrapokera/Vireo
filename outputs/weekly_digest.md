## The number that matters

**13.0% of tickets are repeat contacts** — a customer contacting us again
about the same issue within 30 days of resolution (policy §10). That cost **₹380,050
over this export** at channel contact rates (₹260 per repeat contact on average).
Cutting it to 9% removes roughly **₹17,636 per quarter**
of re-handling cost. The desk has actually operated at 9.3% in its best 13-week window, so the target is proven achievable.

Supporting spend in the same period: **₹347,200** in automatic SLA-breach
credits (₹350 each, policy §3) and **₹249,185** in transfer re-handling (₹305 each, §4).
