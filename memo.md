problem within 30 days.** In this export that is 1,460 avoidable contacts, and at the contact
costs in your own policy document it adds up to about **₹3.8 lakh over 18 months** — roughly

## What people complain about

The digest (attached, refreshed with one command) sorts every ticket by what the customer
actually said in their opening message — not just the tag the intake bot assigned. The
top repeat-offender queues are **Delivery & Shipping, Billing & Payments and Returns & Refunds**.
A third of "Other" tickets are really invoice/payment or delivery problems — the bot is
mis-filing them, which hides them from your category reports.
